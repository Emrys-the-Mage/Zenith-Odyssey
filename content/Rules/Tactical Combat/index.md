##### Slowing Terrain
Exiting a space that is **Slowing Terrain** costs the same as moving 2 spaces.
##### Hazards
Hazards are spaces on the battlefield that can inflict damage or conditions to those who move into them. A character is affected by a Hazard when they enter its space or start their turn there.

### Attacks & Damage
##### Attacks
- Attack Rolls 1d20 vs targets EVASION
- Melee (Reach) vs Ranged (Range).
- Ranged gains **+2 Banes** on attack roll when adjacent to enemy
##### Critical Hits
When you score a **Critical Hit**, the attack automatically hits regardless of the final result. Additionally, all dice used to calculate the damage dealt by this attack are treated as if they had rolled their maximum possible values.
##### Taking Damage
When you take damage, you reduce your HP by the amount of damage taken. 

If this would cause you to reach 0HP or a negative HP, your HP is set to 0 and you gain the [[Conditions & Statuses#Defeated|DEFEATED]] status. Additionally, you gain a **Scar**.
##### Modifying Damage
There are various effects that would modify the amount of damage you would take, either by increasing or decreasing it. The order these effects take place matters. To work out what goes before what, use the following order:
1. [[Keywords#Aegis|Aegis]], which ignores all other effects if it is used.
2. [[Conditions & Statuses#Strength|STRENGTH]] / [[Conditions & Statuses#Weak|WEAK]]
3. Any unmentioned effects that would increase the damage.
4. Adept's [[Character Creation/Adepts/index#Focus Dodge|Focus Dodge]] or Prophet's [[Character Creation/Prophets/index#Divine Intervention|Divine Intervention]]
5. [[Conditions & Statuses#Vulnerable|VULNERABLE]] / [[Conditions & Statuses#Resistant|RESISTANT]]
6. [[Keywords#Armour|Armour]]
7. Any unmentioned effects that would decrease the damage.
8. Mage's [[Character Creation/Mages/index#Hex Barrier|Hex Barrier]]
9. [[Keywords#Shield|Shield]]
##### Persistent Damage
When a character has taken **Persistent Damage**, they immediately take damage equal to the amount of **Persistent Damage**. 

Then, they must roll a **WIT** save at the end of each of their turns. On a failure, they take they take damage equal to the amount of **Persistent Damage**. On a success, they clear all **Persistent Damage**.

When a character takes **Persistent Damage** and is already affected by another source of **Persistent Damage**, the values stack and the highest Save Target is taken.

**Persistent Damage** is unaffected by [[Conditions & Statuses#VULNERABLE|VULNERABLE]], [[Conditions & Statuses#Weak|WEAK]], and [[Keywords#Armour|Armour]].
### Saves
- Rolling 1's and 20's (auto-fail and auto-success respectively. Roll against Save Target.)
### Movement
##### Falling
When you would fall a distance greater than 2 elevation levels, you begin falling. At the end of your next turn (or this turn if you begin falling on your turn), you fall to the lowest possible elevation. For each elevation level you had fallen, you take **2d6 damage**. 
##### Flight
When flying, you ignore all **Hazards** and **Slowing Terrain** that would take effect by stepping on it. Additionally, moving to a space of higher elevation does not cost any additional movement.
##### Voluntary & Involuntary Movement
Some movement is considered voluntary, while sometimes you are forced to move. If an effect from another character forces you to move, this counts as involuntary movement. Otherwise, the movement counts as voluntary.

Some effects in the game are only triggered by either voluntary or involuntary movement.
##### Teleportation
Teleportation is a special kind of movement that transports a character from where they are directly to their destination, ignoring any obstacles in their path. Teleportation does not trigger any Reactions that are triggered by movement.

If a character is [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]], they are unable to teleport unless the effect causing the teleportation explicitly says so.
