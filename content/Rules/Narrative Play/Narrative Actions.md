## Actions
### Aim
When you try to throw or shoot something from a distance, roll **Finesse**. If you can take your time or your target is stationary, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you hit it. On a **Partial Success**, you hit it but suffer a Consequence. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a Consequence.

**Consequences** (your choice).
- The projectile misses or ricochets, hitting something you really wish you didn't hit.
- You gain unwanted attention and reveal your location.
- Your target returns fire, inflicting **Stress** onto you depending on how dangerous they are.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Brawl
When you try to fight someone, roll **Might**. If you can exploit a weakness of theirs or use the environment to your advantage, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, progress the foe's **Defeat Clock** by **1 Segment**. On a **Partial Success**, you progress the **Clock** but suffer one of the **Consequences** below. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a consequence.

**Consequences** (Storymaster's choice).
- The foe fights back, inflicting harm upon you (be it in the form of **Stress**, **Strain**, or other negative effect).
- The foe calls for backup or pulls out a weapon.
- The foe escapes.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.

**Note:** The amount of **Segments** on a foe's **Defeat Clock** will depend on how tough they are. The toughest of foes may have a 6 or an 8-clock. Weak foes may only have a 1-clock (and so are defeated immediately). 
### Commune
When you want to channel or call upon a God, Spirit, or another supernatural entity, you may attempt to **Commune** with them. When you do so, consult the questions below. If you cannot answer "yes" to any of them, your attempt to **Commune** fails.
- As part of your **Communing**, have you sacrificed something to the entity that it would want?
- Have you earned the entity's favour?
- Are you in a place significant to the entity?

If you answered one of them with a "yes", roll **Presence**. If you answered multiple with a "yes", roll with **+1 Boon**. On a **Success**, choose a Blessing below. On a **Partial Success**, choose a Blessing, but suffer a Consequence. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a Consequence.

**Blessings** (your choice).
- You ask the entity for guidance, and it will provide it.
- The entity empowers you, granting **+1 Boon** on all checks for a scene of your choice before the end of the Quest.
- The entity watches over you, allowing you to ignore a Consequence once before the end of a Quest.

**Consequences** (Storymaster's choice).
- This entity will never answer your call again. Automatically fail any further attempts to **Commune** with them.
- The entity is displeased, take **1 Strain**.
- The entity asks a favour of you in exchange for its Blessing. On a **Failure**, this favour is particularly costly.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Comprehend
When you attempt to understand what lies before you, or recall information you may know, roll **Wit**. If you have access to materials or an expert that may help you, gain **+1 Boon**.

In character, ask the Storymaster a question about the situation. Once asked, the question cannot be asked by you or another party member again.

On a **Failure**, the Storymaster must refuse to answer. Otherwise they must provide an answer, though it does not come for free. On a **Success**, choose one of the following. On a **Partial Success**, you must choose two.
- The answer is vague.
- The Storymaster must secretly roll **1d6**, giving a false answer on a 1 or a 2.
- The answer, or how you obtained the answer, haunts you. Take **2 Stress**. How does it frighten you of the world? How does it frighten you of yourself?
- You only make progress towards an answer. Progress a **4-Clock** by 1 segment to uncover the answer. You may ask this question again at another time, and need not pick additional drawbacks. 
### Flex
When you try to move something heavy or break something, roll **Might**. If you can use the environment to your advantage or your emotions are high, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you move or break the thing. On a **Partial Success**, you move or break the thing, but suffer a Consequence below. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a Consequence.

**Consequences** (your choice).
- You gain unwanted attention.
- You are injured, taking **Stress** depending on the danger of the situation.
- You break something else you didn't mean to.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Help
When an ally is performing an Action and you can describe a reasonable way you can increase their chances of success, you may Help them.

When helping an ally, you may do one of the following:
- Grant **+1 Boon** on their roll.
- Make a formerly impossible action now possible.
- Reduce the severity of any possible Consequences. This cannot reduce a Consequence to nothing.

When you Help an ally and they suffer consequences from their Action, you suffer the same Consequence.
### Improvise
When you want to do something risky that isn't covered by another Action, you may Improvise. Describe what you want to do, then roll either **Might**, **Finesse**, **Wit**, or **Presence** (choose the most appropriate). If you can describe how you can use something to your advantage on this Action, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you achieve what you were trying to do. On a **Partial Success**, you either achieve what you wanted to do but to a lesser extent, or you achieve it but suffer a Consequence. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a Consequence.

Consequences on this Action should reflect what was being attempted, and may be suggested by either the Player or the Storymaster. The Storymaster has final say on the Consequence. Basic Consequences include:
- Taking **Stress**
- Gaining **+1 Bane** on further actions.
- Losing equipment or resources.
- Progressing a **Clock** that works against you.
### Inspire
Once per session, you may have a heart to heart with another character. Roll **Presence**. If you take one of their **Stress**, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, choose two of the following. On a **Partial Success**, choose one. 
- They clear **1 Stress**.
- They gain **+1 Boon** on their next roll.
- They may increase their lowest attribute by 1 for the rest of the scene.
### Negotiate with Fate
Before making a roll, you may first **Negotiate with Fate**. In exchange for **+1 Boon**, increased effectiveness, or some other benefit, propose a **drawback** to the **Storymaster**. Example drawbacks include:
- **Worsened** Consequences on a **Failure**/**Partial Success**.
- **Guaranteed** Consequences, in addition to any gained from the Action.
- You lose an important item, through sacrifice or misfortune.
- Introduce a new complication to the scene, such as back up arriving for the bad guys, putting an NPC in danger, or some other unfortunate event.

The **Storymaster** can then accept your bargain, propose one of their own, or decline.
### Protect
When an ally would take **Stress** or **Strain**, you may step in to lessen the blow. Roll **Might**. On a **Success**, you may redistribute the **Stress** or **Strain** your ally would have taken between the two of you as you wish. On a **Partial Success**, take **1 Stress** then proceed as if on a **Success**.
### Push Beyond
When making a roll, you may take **2 Stress** to grant yourself **+1 Boon**.
### Sense
When you want to read a person or spot easily missable details in a situation, roll **WIT**. 

On a **Success**, ask the Storymaster two questions from the list below. On a **Partial Success**, ask one. 
- What here is not as it seems?
- How is this person feeling in this moment?
- What here is of value to me?
- What has happened here recently?
- What does this person want?
### Sneak
When you try to ensure others fail to notice you, roll **Finesse**. If they are distracted or you are particularly concealed, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, they are completely unaware of your presence. On a **Partial Success**, you go unnoticed for now but suffer a **Consequence** below. On a **Failure**, you are spotted.

**Consequences** (your choice).
- You become separated from your allies, or otherwise end up where you don't want to be.
- Drop something important to you that you are unable to retrieve without being caught.
- Fear grows within you. Take **1 Stress**. What awaits you if you are caught?
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Sway
When you try to convince someone of something and they may resist, roll **Presence**. If you offer them something they want or threaten them with something they don't want, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you learn exactly what you would need to say in order to sway them. On a **Partial Success**, you learn exactly what you would need to say, but it will likely incur one of the consequences below. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a consequence.

**Consequences** (Storymaster's choice).
- This character likes you less than they did before.
- This character asks something of you in return, be it a favour, information, **Essence**, or an item of yours.
- The awkwardness inflicts **1 Stress** onto you.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Swift
When you want to move or react quickly, roll **Finesse**. If you can use your environment to your advantage or you are particularly desperate, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you do it in time. On a **Partial Success**, you do it in time but suffer a Consequence. On a **Failure**, you aren't fast enough and suffer a Consequence.

**Consequences** (your choice).
- You crash into something, inflicting **2 Stress** onto you.
- You break something important to you.
- You are shaken, take **+1 Bane** to your next roll.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.
### Tinker
When you want to spend time to try to build a new device or alter an existing one, roll **Wit**. If you have special gear or access to instructions, gain **+1 Boon**.

On a **Success**, you may progress the **Tinker Clock** for this device. On a **Partial Success**, you advance the clock but suffer a Consequence. On a **Failure**, you simply suffer a Consequence and are unable to achieve your goal for now.

**Consequences** (Storymaster's choice).
- Sparks fly, inflicting an amount of **Stress** onto you depending on the danger of the device.
- Your equipment is damaged. You gain **+1 Bane** on Tinker rolls until you **Rest**. What breaks? How are you going to have to fix it later?
- Your creation/alteration doesn't work exactly how you intended, or it has an unusual side effect.
- Progress a **Clock** that works against you.

**Note:**
The **Device Clock** has a number of segments depending on the complexity of the project. Simple projects such as picking a lock may only have 1 segment (and so are completed immediately), while others may have up to 8 segments.
