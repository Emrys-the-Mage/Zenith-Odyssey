Each Downtime, you may do the following:
- Clear all **Stress**.
- Reset your **Bag**.
- **Level Up**.
- Take 2 **Downtime Actions.**
### Downtime Actions
**CRAFT**  
You spend time crafting an item. If you are beginning to craft a new item, spend the **Essence** required. Then, roll an appropriate Skill Check and advance the clock per the success level.

| **Success Level** | **Clock Segments** |
| :---------------- | :----------------: |
| Critical Success  |         3          |
| Success           |         2          |
| Partial Success   |         1          |
| Failure           |         0          |
**FIND INFORMATION**  
You attempt to seek out the answer to a question you have. Describe what you are trying to find out, and how you intend to do so. Roll an appropriate skill, and the Storymaster will describe the outcome.

**ODD JOBS**  
You find work at your current settlement. Describe the work that you find, and roll an appropriate skill. You gain Essence according to the success level.

| **Success Level** |   **Essence**   |
| :---------------- | :-------------: |
| Critical Success  | **3 \|\| 4 \|\| 5** |
| Success           | **2 \|\| 3 \|\| 4** |
| Partial Success   | **1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3** |
| Failure           |        **0**        |
**RELAX**  
You spend time looking after yourself. You may reduce your current **Strain** by **1d4**.

**SEEK TREATMENT**  
You seek aid in treating a **Scar** of yours. Describe an NPC you find to help you, and give them any amount of **Essence**. Roll a number of **d6**'s equal to one more than the **Essence** spent, taking the highest result. Depending on the result, you may advance your *Healing Clock* for that **Scar**.

|  **Result**  | **Clock Segments** |
| :----------: | :----------------: |
| Multiple 6's |         3          |
|      6       |         2          |
|     4-5      |         1          |
|     1-3      |         0          |
**SHOP**  
You search around for a specific item you wish to buy. This item may not be an artefact.

**WORK ON A PROJECT**  
You work on a project of yours. This could mean many things, but some examples include deciphering a code you have found or earning the trust of an NPC travelling with you.

Roll an appropriate Skill Check and advance a clock for the project. The amount of segments on the clock depend on the project's complexity, as determined by the Storymaster. 

| **Success Level** | **Clock Segments** |
| :---------------- | :----------------: |
| Critical Success  |         3          |
| Success           |         2          |
| Partial Success   |         1          |
| Failure           |         0          |
