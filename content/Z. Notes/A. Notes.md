### General Rework Ideas
##### Loot
Break into (rename them):
- **Consumables.** As is.
- **Minor Artefacts.** Small passive bonuses. E.g. -1 Crit Threshold.
- **Major Artefacts.** Each have rich lore and sideplot potential. The owner must complete a side quest (sacrifice something to it, spend time deciphering its language, etc). They only learn of what it does upon completion. It is locked to that player. Narrative items that, like CAIN, use dice pools based on a series of questions.
Lock Artefacts to a player once equipped, to avoid swapping around.

Some minor artefacts that boost some stats but nerf others. Like trade HIT for ST, etc

##### Curse of Lycanthropy
You have contracted lycanthropy, a curse that blurs the line between person and beast. 

There are various strains of lycanthropy, many of which remain undiscovered. Each variant shapes those infected into monstrous people with traits of a particular animal. When you take this Talent, you may choose which strain you have been infected with. Common strains include wolves, bears, rats, boars, and tigers.

Start a permanent Clock for your character, titled ***Caging the Beast***. This clock has 12 segments.
