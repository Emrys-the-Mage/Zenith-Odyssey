### Ideas
- Each needs to come with an associated difficulty modifier
- Bonus Actions
- More turns per round
##### Chilling
When a character ends their turn adjacent to a character with Chilling, they gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Slowed|SLOWED]] until the end of their next turn.
##### Elite I
A character with Elite I gains a **+1 Bonus** to their **HIT, EVASION, Save Target**, and saves.
##### Elite II
A character with Elite I gains a **+2 Bonus** to their **HIT, EVASION, Save Target**, and saves.
##### Elite III
A character with Elite I gains a **+3 Bonus** to their **HIT, EVASION, Save Target**, and saves.
##### Infernal
When a character with Infernal hits with an attack, the target takes **1 || 2 || 3** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]].
##### Floating
A Floating character ignores **Slowing Terrain** and **Hazards** that would require them to step on it to take effect. Additionally, they gain a **+1 Bonus** to their **SPEED**.
##### Tough
A character with Tough gains **1 || 2 || 3 Armour**.
##### Undead
When an Undead character would become [[Conditions & Statuses#Defeated|DEFEATED]], roll a d6. On a 5+, the character does not become [[Conditions & Statuses#Defeated|DEFEATED]] and returns to **1 HP**.
