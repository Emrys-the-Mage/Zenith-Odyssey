##### Many Folk
The **Horde** takes double damage from effects acting on multiple spaces the **Horde** occupies.

### Actions
##### Envelop
*Minor Action*  
The **Horde** envelops an adjacent character at least 2 Sizes smaller than itself. The **Horde** moves so that the target completely within the **Horde's** area, surrounded by spaces the **Horde** occupies on all sides.  

While enveloped, the target becomes [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]], [[Conditions & Statuses#Off-Guard|OFF-GUARD]], and [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded|BLINDED]]. The target may attempt to escape with a successful **FINESSE** save as a Minor Action, moving into a space adjacent to the **Horde**.

The **Horde** may end the envelopment at any time by moving away.
##### Many Attacks
*Minor Action, Melee Attack, Reach 1*  
If the target of this attack is enveloped (as above), the attack is made with **+1 Boon**. On a hit, the target takes **1d6 || 1d8 || 1d10** damage a number of times equal to the **Horde's** Size.
