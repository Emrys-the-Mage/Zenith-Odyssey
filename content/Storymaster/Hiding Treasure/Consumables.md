### Usage
All consumables require a Minor Action to use. You may only use the consumable on yourself unless the consumable has the *Splash* tag, in which case you may administer it to another character within Range 3. If that character is unwilling, they may avoid the effect with a successful **FINESSE** save.

Unless otherwise stated, all consumables are expended after a single use.
### Crafting
To craft a consumable, you first must acquire its recipe. Recipes can be researched, bought, or found in loot.

Once the recipe is acquired, you may begin crafting the consumable by taking the **Craft** Downtime Action or the **Craft** Rest Action. When you do so, use the craft value listed for the consumable to determine the number of segments for the crafting clock, and pay the amount of Essence listed.

Once the crafting clock is complete, you have finished crafting the consumable!
### Purchasing
Default prices for consumables are equal to the Essence cost plus half the Craft value.
