##### Gadgetry
You construct multiple gadgets to enhance yourself or your allies. Choose three options from the list of gadgets below, which you may equip yourself or give to an ally. You may choose the same option multiple times, but cannot equip the same option onto the same character multiple times.

**AUTO DEFIBRILLATOR**  
After becoming [[Conditions & Statuses#DEFEATED|DEFEATED]], roll **1d6** at the end of your next turn. On a **4+**, you clear [[Conditions & Statuses#Defeated|DEFEATED]] and regain **1 HP**.

**FOG GOGGLES**  
You ignore soft cover caused by fog, smoke, or similar effects. 

**GRAPPLING HOOK**  
You gain a new weapon, the grappling hook. It is a ranged weapon with Range 6, deals **1d4 || 2d4 || 3d4** damage on a hit, and allows you move to a space adjacent to the target when you hit. You may target terrain and objects strong enough to hold your weight with this weapon (such as building walls, boulders, trees, etc).

**SPRING SHOES**  
You attach springs to a pair of shoes, granting **+2 SPEED**.
