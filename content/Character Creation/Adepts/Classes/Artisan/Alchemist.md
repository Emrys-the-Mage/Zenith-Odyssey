*Tier II: Blank*   
*Tier III: Blank*   
##### Combine Reactants
When you use a **Trick** with the Alchemy tag, you may spend **4 Focus Dice** to combine its effects with another **Trick** with the Alchemy tag. These **Focus Dice** are not rolled when determining the Success Level of the Tricks. If the second **Trick** requires you to Sacrifice **Focus Dice**, you must do so as normal.

The second Trick must take a Minor Action to use. Both **Tricks** must target the same character or space.
##### Final Adjustments
After rolling **Focus Dice** to use a **Trick** and observing the result, you may choose to spend one more **Focus Die** and roll it for this **Trick**.
