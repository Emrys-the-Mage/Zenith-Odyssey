#### Features
##### Mind Sunder
You gain a new Weapon, in the form of your ability to inflict mental wounds on others. You wield this Weapon at all times, and are able to equip other physical Weapons as usual. This Weapon is a Ranged Weapon with Range 10, it ignores all **cover**, and deals **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** damage on a hit. Additionally, when a character is hit with this weapon, they gain **+1 Bane** on their next **WIT** or **PRESENCE** save.
##### Mind Reading
You gain the [[Miracles#Reading|Reading]] **Miracle**, but can only gain its Standard Effect.
