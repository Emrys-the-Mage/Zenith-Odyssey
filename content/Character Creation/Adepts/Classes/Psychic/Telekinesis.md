#### Features
##### Psychic Force
You gain a new Weapon in the form of your ability to exert your telekinetic will upon others. You wield this Weapon at all times, and are able to equip other physical Weapons as usual. This weapon is a Ranged Weapon with Range 8, has [[Keywords#Knockback|Knockback]] **3 || 4 || 5**, and deals **1d8 || 2d8 || 3d8** damage on a hit. Additionally, you ignore penalties on using this Weapon when an enemy is adjacent to you.
##### Telelevitation
While in combat, you levitate above the ground. You can fly, and you ignore hazards and slowing terrain that would require you to step on them. Additionally, you gain a **+1 Bonus** to your **EVASION**.
