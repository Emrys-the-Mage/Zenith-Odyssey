#### Features
##### Dazzle
When you would deal damage with your Sneaky Attack feature, you may instead spend **1 Focus Die** to inflict [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded|BLINDED]] on the target.
##### Distracting Taunt
When you successfully *Taunt* a character, the target also becomes [[Conditions & Statuses#Off-Guard|OFF-GUARD]] until the end of your next turn.
##### Untraceable
You are immune to the [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked|MARKED]] condition.
