**Description**  
Masters of stealth and edge, **Shades** abuse their ability to go unnoticed and hit their enemies where it hurts most.

**Playstyle**  
Shades shine brightest when enveloped in darkness. To be most effective, you want to be [[Conditions & Statuses#Hidden|HIDDEN]] as much as possible. To effectively *Hide*, make good use of cover and the [[Conditions & Statuses#Invisible|INVISIBLE]] status.

**Complexity:** ★★★☆☆

#### Tricks
You learn 3 **Tricks** of your choice. Every odd level, you learn a new **Trick**. Additionally, each time you increase in Tier you may learn an additional **Trick**.
#### Features
##### Sneaky Attack
When you make an attack while [[Conditions & Statuses#Hidden|HIDDEN]] or when you expend [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked|MARKED]] on the target for the attack, the attack deals an additional **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** on a hit.
##### Lurker
At the end of each of your turns, you may *Hide* as a Free Action.
