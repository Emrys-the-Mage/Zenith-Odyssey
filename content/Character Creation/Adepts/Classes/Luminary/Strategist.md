#### Features
##### Flash of Genius
When you roll dice for the success level of a **Trick**, set aside any 1's you roll and reroll them once. These dice are still treated as 1's for the success level of the **Trick**, but use their new values for your Guiding Light feature.
##### Calculate Weakness
You gain the [[Adept Combat Talents#Reveal Weakness|Reveal Weakness]] Talent, without it counting towards the number of Talents you know.
