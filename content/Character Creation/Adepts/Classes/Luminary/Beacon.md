#### Features
##### Encouraging Words
When an ally within Range 5 fails a save, you may spend your Reaction and a **2 Focus Dice** to provide encouraging words. Make a **PRESENCE** save against the same Save Target. On a success, it is treated as if your ally had passed their save.
##### Warming Light
When you use your Guiding Light feature on an ally, you lend them a d6. After they make an attack roll or save but before they learn if it was successful, they may choose to spend the d6 and add it to the roll. They then return the d6 to you. This d6 is separate from **Boons** and **Banes**.
