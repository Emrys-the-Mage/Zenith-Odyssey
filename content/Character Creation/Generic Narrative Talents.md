##### Adaptive
When using **Push Beyond**, you may instead take **3 Stress** to roll your best **Attribute**.
##### First Aid
Once per session, when an ally of yours takes **Stress** as a consequence of a Check, if you are able to take a short amount of time to tend to them within the same scene in which they obtained the **Stress**, you may patch it up. When you do so, the **Stress** is cleared.
##### Intimidating
When using **Sway**, you may roll **Might** instead of **Presence**.
##### Laugh it Off
When you fail and would suffer Consequences with **Sway**, you may attempt to pass off what you said as a joke. Roll **1d6**, suffering no Consequences on a 4+. Otherwise, the Consequences are twice as bad.
##### Side Hustle
At the end of every session, gain **1 Essence**.
##### Reckless
When using **Brawl**, you may take **2 Stress** to progress your opponent's Defeat Clock by an additional segment, regardless of the outcome of your check.
##### Researcher
When you make a Check for a Rest Action or a Downtime Action to progress a clock to discover something new, whether that's answering a burning question you have, discovering lore through tomes you found, or researching a new Recipe, you may fill in an additional segment of the clock after your Check.
##### Small Friend
You have a small pet that you bring with you on your adventures, providing moral support. You may increase your **Stress Cap** by 1. The pet is solely useful at providing moral support, not aiding you in other activities.
##### Summon Weapon
Your Weapons are magically bound to you. You may summon them to your hand or dismiss them to a pocket dimension at any time.
##### Supernatural Charm
*Prerequisite: [[Ancestry#Faeblood|Faeblood]]*  
You may spend **2 Stress** to magically enhance your charm. For the rest of the scene, you gain **+1 Boon** on all Command, Sway, and Perform checks.
