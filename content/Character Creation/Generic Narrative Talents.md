### Tier I
##### Curse of Vampirism
You have been cursed to live your life with an unholy lust for blood, becoming an undead known as a vampire.

You no longer need to eat, nor do you need to drink water. However, you are now required to regularly drink blood, not necessarily that of people, to sustain yourself.

Start a permanent Clock for your character, titled ***Bloodlust***. This clock has 10 segments.

When making any **Skill Check**, ask yourself: *"Can I see or smell **fresh blood** in this moment?"* (here the blood must be from something like an open wound, not blood that is currently within a creature). If you answer yes, tick your ***Bloodlust*** clock by 1 segment. Then, you gain **+1 Boon** to the check if the Skill is associated with **MIGHT** or **FINESSE**, or **+1 Bane** if it's associated with **WIT** or **PRESENCE**.  

When you fill your ***Bloodlust*** clock, your cravings take control. Choose one of the following options:
- **Stay.** You stay in the scene, but must immediately and constantly take violent actions against other characters with the sole goal to **drink**. This lasts until you do so, or until the next scene. Should you drink from another PC, they take a **Strain**.
- **Leave.** You leave the scene in an effort to protect those around you so that you may instead hunt for wildlife or other creatures you can find. You return in the following scene.

Regardless of your choice, the emotional trauma of losing control inflicts a **Scar**.

Your ***Bloodlust*** clock is reset to 0 in between Quests.
##### "I Won't Let You Down"
You are driven to ensure you are seen as reliable. When another player spends **Stress** to help you on a **Skill Check** you are making, a result of a 2 or a 3 counts as a Partial Success.
##### Researcher
When you make a Skill Check to progress a clock to learn a recipe, you may fill in an additional segment of the clock.
##### Small Friend
You have a small pet that you bring with you on your adventures, providing moral support. You may increase your **Stress Cap** by 1.
##### Summon Weapon
Your Weapons are magically bound to you. You may summon them to your hand or dismiss them to a pocket dimension at any time.
##### Supernatural Charm
*Prerequisite: [[Generic Combat Talents#Curse of Vampirism|Curse of Vampirism]] or [[Ancestry#Faeblood|Faeblood]]*  
You may spend **2 Stress** to magically enhance your charm. For the rest of the scene, you gain **+1 Boon** on all Command, Sway, and Perform checks.
### Tier II
##### Skill Mastery
*Prerequisite: Tier II*
When increasing your Skills' values, you may do so to a maximum of 4, rather than 3. When you take this Talent, increase one of your Skills by one.
### Tier III
##### Supernatural Skillset
*Prerequisite: Tier III, [[Generic Combat Talents#Skill Mastery|Skill Mastery]]*
When increasing your Skills' values, you may do so to a maximum of 5, rather than 4. When you take this Talent, increase one of your Skills by one.
