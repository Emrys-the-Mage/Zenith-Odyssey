##### Adaptive
When making a **Skill Check**, you may take **3 Stress** to instead roll a different Skill of your choice.
##### First Aid
Once per session, when an ally of yours takes a **Strain** as a consequence of a Skill Check, if you are able to take a short amount of time to tend to them within the same scene in which they obtained the **Strain**, you may patch it up. When you do so, the **Strain** is cleared.
##### I Won't Let You Down
When another player spends **Stress** to help you on a **Skill Check** you are making, a result of a 2 or a 3 counts as a Partial Success.
##### Side Hustle
At the end of every session, gain **1 Essence**.
##### Researcher
When you make a Skill Check to progress a clock to discover something new, whether that's answering a burning question you have, discovering lore through tomes you found, or researching a new Recipe, you may fill in an additional segment of the clock after your Skill Check.
##### Skill Mastery
*Prerequisite: Tier II*  
When increasing your Skills' values, you may do so to a maximum of 4, rather than 3. When you take this Talent, increase one of your Skills by one.
##### Small Friend
You have a small pet that you bring with you on your adventures, providing moral support. You may increase your **Stress Cap** by 1. The pet is solely useful at providing moral support, not aiding you in other activities.
##### Summon Weapon
Your Weapons are magically bound to you. You may summon them to your hand or dismiss them to a pocket dimension at any time.
##### Supernatural Charm
*Prerequisite: [[Ancestry#Faeblood|Faeblood]]*  
You may spend **2 Stress** to magically enhance your charm. For the rest of the scene, you gain **+1 Boon** on all Command, Sway, and Perform checks.
##### Supernatural Skillset
*Prerequisite: Tier III, [[Generic Combat Talents#Skill Mastery|Skill Mastery]]*
When increasing your Skills' values, you may do so to a maximum of 5, rather than 4. When you take this Talent, increase one of your Skills by one.
