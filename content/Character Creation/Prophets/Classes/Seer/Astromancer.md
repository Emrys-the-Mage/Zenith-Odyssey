#### Features
##### Constellations
Choose **1 || 2 || 3** numbers on your Fate Die. When you roll one of these numbers on your Fate Die, you regain **1 Glimpse**.
##### Alignment
When you gain the **Divine Effect** of a **Miracle**, you may immediately inflict [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked|MARKED]] onto a character within Range 10.
