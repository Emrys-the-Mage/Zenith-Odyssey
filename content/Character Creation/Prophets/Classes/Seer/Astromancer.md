#### Description
###### Example Titles
*Star Child, Sky Guide*
#### Features
##### Constellations
Choose **1 || 2 || 3** numbers on your Fate Die. When you roll one of these numbers on your Fate Die, you regain **1 Glimpse**.
##### Alignment
When you roll a **d20** for an attack or save and the result on the **d20** matches one of your remaining **Glimpses**, you immediately gain a **+3 Bonus** to the roll.
