#### Description
###### Example Titles
*Emissary of the Dead, Soulkeeper of Aleson*
#### Features
##### Usher Toward Death
Twice per round when a character within Range 10 has their [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked|MARKED]] condition consumed, you may deal **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** damage to them as a Reaction.
##### Whispers of the Fallen
When an enemy within Range 10 is [[Conditions & Statuses#Defeated|DEFEATED]], you may immediately use the [[Miracles#Reading|Reading]] **Miracle** as a Reaction.
