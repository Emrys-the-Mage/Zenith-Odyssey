#### Description
###### Example Titles
*Obsessed Chronomancer, Temporal Scholar, She who is lost in time*
#### Features
##### Peak into the Future
Before using a **Miracle**, you may spend a **Glimpse** to view two possible outcomes. Roll your Fate Die twice, choosing either result for the **Miracle**.
##### Foresee Danger
You gain a **+1 Bonus** to your **EVASION**, **FINESSE** saves, and **WIT** saves.
