#### Features
##### Wise Teachings
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Miracles#CLEANSE|Cleanse]]* and *[[Miracles#Envigour|Envigour]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Dispense Wisdom
When an ally within Range 5 is about to make a save, you may use your Reaction to grant them **+1 Boon** on the save.
##### Restraint
For every **4 Grace** you have, the Fate Target of your **Miracles** is reduced by 1. A Fate Target cannot be reduced below 2. When you spend or lose **Grace**, the Fate Targets are adjusted according to your current amount of **Grace**.
