#### Features
##### Glorious Teachings
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Blessing|Blessing]]* and *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#REVITALISE|Revitalise]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Benedictions
You gain a pool of **3 || 5 || 7** d8's at the start of every combat. You may use your Reaction to expend them in the following ways:
- When an ally within Range 8 would take damage, you may expend any amount of the dice and roll them to reduce the damage taken by the amount rolled.
- When an ally within Range 8 misses an attack roll, you may expend a single die to roll it and add it to the result, potentially turning it into a hit.
