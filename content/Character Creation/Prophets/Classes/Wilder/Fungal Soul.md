#### Wheel of Life: Give & Take
Your Wheel of Life embodies the ebb and flow of claiming nutrients from your surroundings during Growth and returning those nutrients during Decay. Your Wheel has these phases, Growth and Decay, in the following order with the following number of segments. You begin combat in **Growth**.

| **Phase** | **Segments** | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                              |
| :-------- | :----------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Growth    |      3       | You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Weak\|WEAK]] 1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3 and [[Conditions & Statuses#Resistant\|RESISTANT]] 2 \|\| 4 \|\| 6 while you are in this phase.           |
| Decay     |      3       | You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Strength\|STRENGTH]] 2 \|\| 4 \|\| 6 and [[Conditions & Statuses#Vulnerable\|VULNERABLE]] 1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3 while you are in this phase. |
| Growth    |      3       | You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Weak\|WEAK]] 1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3 and [[Conditions & Statuses#Resistant\|RESISTANT]] 2 \|\| 4 \|\| 6 while you are in this phase.           |
| Decay     |      3       | You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Strength\|STRENGTH]] 2 \|\| 4 \|\| 6 and [[Conditions & Statuses#Vulnerable\|VULNERABLE]] 1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3 while you are in this phase. |
#### Features
##### Gifts of the Mycelium
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Miracles#Decay|Decay]]* and *[[Miracles#Envigour|Envigour]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Noxious Spores
When you would take damage (before all kinds of damage reduction), you may as a Reaction inflict 2 || 4 || 6 [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]] to a target within Range 3.
