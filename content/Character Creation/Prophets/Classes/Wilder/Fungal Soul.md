#### Wheel of Life: Give & Take
Your Wheel of Life embodies the ebb and flow of claiming nutrients from your opponents during Decay, and gifting them to your allies during Growth. Your Wheel has these phases, Growth and Decay, in the following order with the following number of segments. You begin combat in **Growth**.

| **Phase** | **Segments** | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                       |
| :-------- | :----------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Growth    |      3       | Twice per round when an ally within Range 4 is hit by an attack, You may use your Reaction to roll **1d6** and subtract it from the attack roll, potentially causing it to miss. |
| Decay     |      3       | Twice per round when an ally within Range 4 hits with an attack, you may use a Reaction to inflict **1d6 \|\| 2d6 \|\| 3d6 damage** on the target.                               |
| Growth    |      3       | As above.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Decay     |      3       | As above.                                                                                                                                                                        |
#### Features
##### Gifts of the Mycelium
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Decay|Decay]]* and *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Envigour|Envigour]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Noxious Spores
When you would take damage (before all kinds of damage reduction), you may as a Reaction inflict **2 || 4 || 6** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]] to a target within Range 3.
