**Description**  
Heralds of the natural world, **Wilders** call for spirits, plants, and animals to aid their allies and hinder their foes.

**Playstyle**  
Wilders use their Feathered Friend to deliver Miracles from safety. An effective Wilder is essentially present in two places at once.

**Complexity:** ★★★★★

#### Miracles
You learn 3 **Miracles** of your choice. Each time you increase in Tier, you learn an additional 4 **Miracles**.
#### Features
##### Wheel of Life
Each Wilder moves through Phases of a clock, known as their Wheel of Life. The Phases of your Wheel of Life, as well as their effects, are detailed in your Subclass.

Each Wheel of Life is a clock with 12 segments. To represent this, draw a circle and divide it into 12. You then place a token onto the first segment of the clock, representing where you are in the cycle. This will be the first segment of your first Phase, detailed in your Subclass.

At the end of each of your turns, roll **1d4** and move that many segments clockwise around your Wheel of Life, potentially altering which Phase you are in.
#### Specialty
When building a Wilder, you choose a branch of life you most closely identify with. Choose one of the following, gaining its features:
- [[Beast Heart]], for those who form close bonds to animals.
- [[Verdant Spirit]], for those who cultivate plant life.
- [[Fungal Soul]], for those who facilitate growth and decay.
