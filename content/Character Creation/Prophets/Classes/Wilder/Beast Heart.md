###### Example Titles
#### Wheel of Life: Animalia
Your Wheel of Life embodies the various types of animals, your essence flowing between them. Your Wheel has the following phases, in the listed order with their respective segments and effects. You begin combat in your **Mammal** phase.

| **Phase** | **Segments** | **Effect**                                                                                                                        |
| :-------- | :----------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mammal    |      2       | Your **HIT** is increased by 1.                                                                                                   |
| Bird      |      2       | While in this phase, you can **fly** and your **SPEED** is increased by 1.                                                        |
| Reptile   |      2       | The number you need to roll for your **Divine Intervention** is reduced by 1.                                                     |
| Amphibian |      2       | When you are hit with a melee attack, the attacker takes **1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage\|Persistent Damage]].   |
| Fish      |      2       | Water fills a Size 2 Circle centred on you, becoming **Slowing Terrain**. This circle moves with you, and you ignore its effects. |
| Insect    |      2       | Your **EVASION** is increased by 3 and your Size is reduced to 1/2.                                                               |
#### Features
##### Gifts of Feather & Fur
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#EAGLE TOTEM|Eagle Totem]]* and *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Ursine Totem|Ursine Totem]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Feathered Friend
When combat starts, you summon a fae bird to your aid. The bird appears in a space adjacent to you and has the following statistics. 

| **Feathered Friend Statistics** |                   |
| :------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| **HP**                          | = 1               |
| **EVASION**                     | = 14 + your Level |
| **SPEED**                       | = 6 \|\| 7 \|\| 8 |
| **MIGHT save**                  | = -5 + your Level |
| **FINESSE save**                | = 4 + your Level  |
| **WIT save**                    | = Your Level      |
| **PRESENCE save**               | = 1 + your Level  |

Your bird can use a **Standard Move** on your turn, but otherwise takes no actions. You can use your **Miracles** as if you were in your bird's space.  

If your bird is [[Conditions & Statuses#DEFEATED|DEFEATED]], it immediately vanishes. You can call upon your bird again as a Minor Action.
