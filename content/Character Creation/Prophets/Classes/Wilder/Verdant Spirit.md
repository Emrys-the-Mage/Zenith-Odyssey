#### Wheel of Life: Seasons
Your Wheel of Life embodies the cyclical nature of the environment, moving between seasons. Your Wheel of Life has the following phases in the specified order, with the given number of segments. You begin combat in **Spring**.

| **Phase** | **Segments** | **Effect**                                                                                                                                             |
| :-------- | :----------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spring    |      3       | While in this phase at the start of your turn, you may inflict [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked\|MARKED]] onto a target within Range 8 as a Free Action. |
| Summer    |      3       | While in this phase, you and all allies within Range 2 gain **+1 Boon** on all saves.                                                                  |
| Autumn    |      3       | While in this phase, you and all allies within Range 2 gain **soft cover**.                                                                            |
| Winter    |      3       | None.                                                                                                                                                  |
#### Features
##### Gifts of the Branch
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Miracles#Cleanse|Cleanse]]* and *[[Miracles#Summon Roots|Summon Roots]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Retaliating Vines
Whenever a character within line of sight makes an attack against you and hits, they must make a **MIGHT** save or become [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]] until the end of your next turn.
