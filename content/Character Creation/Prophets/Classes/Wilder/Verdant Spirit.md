#### Wheel of Life: Seasons
Your Wheel of Life embodies the cyclical nature of the environment, moving between seasons. Your Wheel of Life has the following phases in the specified order, with the given number of segments. You begin combat in **Spring**.

| **Phase** | **Segments** | **Effect**                                                                                                                            |
| :-------- | :----------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spring    |      3       | At the start of your turn, you may inflict [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked\|MARKED]] onto two targets within Range 8 as a Free Action. |
| Summer    |      3       | You and all allies within Range 2 gain **+1 Boon** on all saves.                                                                      |
| Autumn    |      3       | You and all allies within Range 2 gain **soft cover**.                                                                                |
| Winter    |      3       | At the start of your turn, you may clear up to 2 Conditions on an ally within Range 2 as a Free Action.                               |
#### Features
##### Gifts of the Branch
You gain the **Miracles** *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Cleanse|Cleanse]]* and *[[Character Creation/Prophets/Miracles#Summon Roots|Summon Roots]]* for free, without them counting towards the number of **Miracles** you know.
##### Retaliating Vines
Whenever a character within line of sight makes an attack against you, they must make a **MIGHT** save or become [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]] until the end of your next turn.
