##### Beast Tongue
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Wilder/index|Wilder]]*  
You may spend **2 Stress** to gain the ability to communicate with any non-humanoid animal for the rest of the scene. Animals you speak to are under no obligation to be truthful or helpful towards you and will generally put their own interests above yours. It may be possible however to sway them through successful checks or through bargains.
##### Commune with the Fallen
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Priest/index|Priest]]*  
You may spend **2 Stress** to commune with a corpse of a deceased person. The corpse is granted the ability to answer up to three of your questions, each of which it is only able to answer with "yes", "no", or "unsure". The corpse has access to the knowledge it had in life and is compelled to answer truthfully, however it does not retain the soul of the deceased person. 
##### Divination
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Seer/index|Seer]]*  
At the end of every session, you may ask the Storymaster a single yes or no question. The Storymaster then secretly rolls a d6. On a 3+, they must respond truthfully, otherwise they must lie.
##### Divine Empathy
By glancing at someone, you are able to tell their general current emotional state, regardless of how well they hide it.
##### Precognition
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Seer/index|Seer]]*  
You may briefly peer into the immediate future to sense the severity of the consequences of your actions. You may spend **1 Stress** before making a Check to peer into the future. When you do so, roll the Check as normal, observing the result. You may then decide whether or not to proceed with this course of action, using the observed result if you do.  If you choose not to proceed, you may instead take a different course of action, however the next time you roll that Attribute this scene, you must use the result you had rolled.
##### Rootspeak
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Wilder/index|Wilder]]*  
By placing your hand to the ground at the base of a plant, you may spend **2 Stress** to ask the plant up to three questions, each of which it will do its best to answer truthfully. 

A plant is generally aware of its surroundings and those who pass by it, and what events take place in its presence. Some plants may even be able to ask nearby plants to gather an answer for you.
##### Trustworthy
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Priest/index|Priest]]*  
As a Priest, people tend to trust your word. You gain **+1 Boon** on all Checks made to influence others who are not hostile towards you.
##### Unholy Detection
By spending **1 Stress**, until the end of the scene you gain the ability to detect the presence of undead or demonic creatures, or undead or demonic magic, within about 10 metres.
