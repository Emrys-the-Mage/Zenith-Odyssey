#### Features
##### Undefeatable
When you would reach **0 HP**, you instead regain **10 || 20 || 30 HP** and gain a **Scar**. If you weren't already under the effects of your transformation from your Unleash the Monster feature, you also immediately undergo the transformation.
##### Unleash the Monster
At the start of your turn on Round 3, you undergo a transformation. Until the end of combat, you gain the following effects:
- Increase your **Size** by 1.
- You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Strength|STRENGTH]] **2 || 4 || 6**.
- Your attacks gain [[Keywords#Knockback|Knockback]] **2 || 4 || 6**.
