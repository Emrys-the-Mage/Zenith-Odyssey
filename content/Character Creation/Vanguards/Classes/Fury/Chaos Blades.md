#### Features
##### Explosive Strikes
When you roll a 1 on a die used for calculating the damage you would deal, you immediately reduce your **HP** by 1 and add another die of the same size to your damage calculation.
##### Mishaps
When you are hit with an attack, roll 1d6. The corresponding effect on the below table takes place.

| **1d6** | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                              |
| :-----: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|    1    | No effect.                                                                                                                                                              |
|    2    | No effect.                                                                                                                                                              |
|    3    | The attacker gains [[Conditions & Statuses#Marked\|MARKED]].                                                                                                            |
|    4    | You teleport to a space of your choice within Range 3.                                                                                                                  |
|    5    | Your blades absorb the strike. Decrease the incoming damage by **2 \|\| 4 \|\| 6**, and your next attack deals an additional **1d6 \|\| 2d6 \|\| 3d6** damage on a hit. |
|    6    | You explode, dealing **1d12 \|\| 2d12 \|\| 3d12** damage to yourself and all characters adjacent to you.                                                                |
