#### Features
##### Ruthless
When your Rampage die is a **d8** or higher and you hit with an attack, you may forego adding additional damage from Rampage to instead turn the attack into a **Critical Hit**. When you do so, reset your Rampage die to a **d4**.
##### Open Wounds
When you score a **Critical Hit**, the target also becomes [[Conditions & Statuses#Vulnerable|VULNERABLE]] **3 || 6 || 9** until the start of your next turn.
