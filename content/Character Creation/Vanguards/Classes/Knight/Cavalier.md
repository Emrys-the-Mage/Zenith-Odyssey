#### Features
##### Trusty Steed
You gain an animal companion you ride into battle, your Steed.

While mounted on your Steed, your **Size** becomes **2** and your **SPEED** becomes **6 || 7 || 8**, but each time you move you may only move in a straight line.

You may dismount your Steed as a Free Action, moving to a space adjacent to your Steed.

While not mounted, your Steed does not move. Your Steed is also immune to all damage.
##### Charge!
After moving at least 4 spaces on your turn while on your Steed, your next attack this turn deals an additional **1d8 || 2d8 || 3d8** damage and gains [[Keywords#Knockback|Knockback]] **2 || 4 || 6**.
