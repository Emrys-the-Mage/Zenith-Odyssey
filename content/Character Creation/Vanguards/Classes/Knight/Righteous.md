#### Features
##### Protection Burst
Each time you take damage (even if the damage is reduced to 0), a pulse of protective energy emits from your armour. All characters you choose within Range 4 gain **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** [[Keywords#Shield|Shield]], other than yourself.
##### Righteous Smite
When you hit on an attack and deal damage, you may enhance your strike with divine energy. 

To do so, spend any amount of **Adrenaline** you wish. You then deal an additional **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** damage for each **Adrenaline** spent.  

This damage counts as a separate instance of damage, and is not affected by **Critical Hits**.
