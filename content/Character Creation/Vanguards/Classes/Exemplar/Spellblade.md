#### Features
##### Elemental Stances
When you enter a Stance, you may spend **1 Adrenaline** to imbue the Stance with an element of your choice. Choose from the list below, gaining the corresponding effect.

**FLAME**  
Your next attack deals an additional **1d4 || 2d4 || 3d4** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]] on a hit.

**EARTH**  
You become [[Conditions & Statuses#Resistant|RESISTANT]] **1 || 2 || 3** until the start of your next turn.

**FROST**  
Until the start of your next turn, all spaces within Range 2 of you count as **Slowing Terrain**. You ignore this effect for your movement.

**GUST**  
Until the start of your next turn, you gain a **+2 Bonus** to your **SPEED**.
