#### Features
##### En Guarde
As a Minor Action, you may force a character within Range 5 to accept a duel. You can only duel a given character once per combat.

While duelling, you and the target are unable to take hostile actions towards any characters other each other.

If a character other than you takes a hostile action towards your opponent, the duel ends.

You may end the duel on your turn as a Free Action.
##### Footwork
When you hit with an attack, you may move 2 spaces without provoking Reactions.
##### Perfected Technique
You gain a **+1 bonus** to your **HIT**.
