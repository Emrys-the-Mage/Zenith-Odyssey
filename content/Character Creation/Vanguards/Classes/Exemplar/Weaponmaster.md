#### Features
##### Perfect Weapon
You own a Weapon unlike any other. Take your Vanguard Weapon, and apply two of the following enhancements.

| **Enhancement** | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| :-------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Guided          | The Weapon gains the [[Keywords#Accurate\|Accurate]] tag if it didn't have it already. If the Weapon has the [[Keywords#Inaccurate\|Inaccurate]] tag, you instead remove the [[Keywords#Inaccurate\|Inaccurate]] tag. |
| Heavy           | The Weapon gains [[Keywords#Knockback\|Knockback]] **1 \|\| 2 \|\| 3**.                                                                                                                                               |
| Oversized       | The Weapon gains +1 Reach.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Perfect Edge    | The Weapon gains a **+1 \|\| +2 \|\| +3 Bonus** to damage and gains the [[Keywords#Deadly\|Deadly]] tag.                                                                                                              |
##### Weakspots
You have an intuition for where to find an enemy's weak points, as well as an intimate understanding of your own.

While in your **Offensive Stance**, you reduce your **Crit Threshold** by 1.

While in your **Defensive Stance**, you become immune to **Critical Hits** (they are instead treated as normal hits).
