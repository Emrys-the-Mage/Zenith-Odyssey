#### Features
##### Radiance
You gain a **d6**, called your **Radiance Die**. At the start of a combat, your Radiance Die has the 1 side facing up.

Once on each of your turns when you cast a **Spell** with the Light tag, you may increase the value on the die by 1, to a maximum of 6.

You gain the following benefits, depending on the value of the die.

| **Value** | **Benefit**                                                                                                   |
| :-------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|     1     | No benefit.                                                                                                   |
|     2     | No benefit.                                                                                                   |
|     3     | All characters you choose within Range 1 are [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded\|BLINDED]] while in this area.   |
|     4     | All characters you choose within Range 2 are [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded\|BLINDED]] until they move away. |
|     5     | All characters you choose within Range 3 are [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded\|BLINDED]] until they move away. |
|     6     | All characters you choose within Range 4 are [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded\|BLINDED]] until they move away. |
