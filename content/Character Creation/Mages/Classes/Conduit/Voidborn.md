#### Features
##### Shroud of Magicks
When you cast a **Spell**, you gain **soft cover** until the start of your next turn.

If you cast a **Spell** while you benefit from this **soft cover**, you instead turn [[Conditions & Statuses#Invisible|INVISIBLE]] until the start of your next turn.
