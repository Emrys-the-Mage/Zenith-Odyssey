#### Forbidden Deals
**ASHES TO ASHES**  
You gain [[Conditions & Statuses#Strength|STRENGTH]] **2 || 4 || 6** and [[Conditions & Statuses#Vulnerable|VULNERABLE]] **2 || 4 || 6** until the start of your next turn.

**IMMOLATION CONTRACT**  
Your body is set ablaze. Reduce your **HP** by 1. Before your next turn, any character that hits you with an attack immediately takes **1d8 || 2d8 || 3d8** damage.
#### Features
##### Hellfire
Each time your **HP** is reduced or you block damage with Mana Shield, a **hazard** is created in the space you occupy.

This space is on fire, and deals **1 || 2 || 3** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]]. You are immune to **hazards** you create in this way.
