#### Forbidden Deals
**CURSE OF MISFORTUNE**  
Targets of the **Spells** you cast this turn make all Saves against the **Spells** with **+1 Bane**. You then become [[Conditions & Statuses#Off-Guard|OFF-GUARD]] and [[Conditions & Statuses#MARKED|MARKED]] until the start of your next turn.

**EYE FOR AN EYE**  
You become [[Conditions & Statuses#Invisible|INVISIBLE]] and [[Conditions & Statuses#Blinded|BLINDED]] until the start of your next turn.

##### Coven's Trickery
When you end your turn within Range 5 of an ally, you may swap places with them.
