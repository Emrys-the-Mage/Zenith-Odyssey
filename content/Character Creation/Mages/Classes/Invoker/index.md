**Description**  
Practitioners of the occult, **Invokers** forge contracts with unseen beings of immense magical power.

**Playstyle**  
Invokers balance the curses of their Forbidden Deals with the blessings they grant in order to claw for power otherwise unattainable. An effective Invoker knows how to mitigate such curses.

**Complexity:** ★★★★☆

#### Spells
You learn 4 **Spells** of your choice, and an additional **Spell** each time you gain a Level.
#### Features
##### Forbidden Deals
As an Invoker, you negotiate with unseen magical entities for your power. As a Free Action at the start of any of your turns, you may make a **Forbidden Deal**. You gain access to the **Forbidden Deal** below, as well as the ones granted in your **Specialty** and your Talents.

**STOLEN SWIFTNESS**  
You may take an additional Minor Action on this turn. As soon as this turn ends, you are [[Conditions & Statuses#STUNNED|STUNNED]] and [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]] until the end of your next turn. Additionally, you cannot make a Forbidden Deal next turn.

As an Invoker, you select your branch of old magicks you specialise in. Choose one of the following.
