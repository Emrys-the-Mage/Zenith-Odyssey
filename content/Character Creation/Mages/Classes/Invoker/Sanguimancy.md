#### Forbidden Deals
**BLOOD VOW**  
You forge an oath in blood. Take a **Scar**. Anytime you would be reduced to **0 HP** before the start of your next turn, you are reduced to **1 HP** instead.

**SCARLET SECRETS**  
You sacrifice your blood in exchange for magical energy. Sacrifice any amount of **HP** you wish, gaining **2 Blood Points** for each **HP** sacrificed. This **HP** reduction cannot be negated or reduced in any way.

You may spend **Blood Points** in place of **Mana** when casting a **Spell**. You lose all unspent **Blood Points** at the end of the scene.
#### Features
##### Vitality Extraction
As a Minor Action, you may attempt to consume the vitality of a target within Range 6. The target must make a **MIGHT** save. On a failed save, the target loses **1d6 || 2d6 || 3d6** **HP** and you regain **1 || 2 || 3 HP**.
