##### Rune Application
You know how to inscribe runes on equipment to prepare your party for combat. Outside of combat, you may inscribe two of the Runes below, on your or your allies' equipment. You may choose the same Rune multiple times, but cannot apply the same Rune to the same character multiple times.

**BARRIER RUNE**  
You inscribe this Rune onto garments. On their turn, the wearer may invoke the Rune as a Free Action to gain [[Keywords#Aegis|Aegis]]. When they do so, the Rune dissipates and cannot be used again until reapplied.

**FLAME RUNE**  
You inscribe this Rune onto a weapon. On a hit, the weapon deals an additional **1 || 2 || 3** [[Keywords#Persistent Damage|Persistent Damage]].

**LUCK RUNE**  
You inscribe this Rune onto garments. The Rune has two charges. When the wearer makes an attack roll or save, they may expend a charge to reroll the d20. They must use the new result. When the last charge is expended, the Rune dissipates and cannot be used again until reapplied.
