##### Aether Construct
You may spend **2 Stress** to create a simple non-magical item that can fit within your cupped hands. You cannot use this to create Weapons.
##### Aethersight
By spending **1 Stress**, until the end of the scene you gain the ability to perceive a colourful aura around individuals or objects currently affected by magic.
##### Chanel the Coven
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Mages/Classes/Invoker/index|Invoker]]*  
You form a pact with one of your allies. Any time either of you would roll a **Skill Check**, they may choose to **Invoke the Pact**. When they do so, they may use the other's Skill Value for the check. However, should they then take **Stress** or **Strain** as a result of a consequence to that check, the other also takes the same amount of **Stress** or **Strain**. 
##### I Have the Perfect Spell!
Twice per session, you may help an ally on a **Skill Check** without spending **Stress**. What spell can you cast to help them in this moment?
##### Inner Magicks
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Mages/Classes/Conduit/index|Conduit]]*  
Twice per session, when you spend **2 Stress** to grant yourself **+1 Boon** to a Skill Check, you may also increase the **Reward** of that check by one level.
