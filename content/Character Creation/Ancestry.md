#### Human
*Description Here.*
###### Adaptable
When you make a Skill Check with a Skill in which you have 0 dice, you may spend **2 Stress** to gain **+2 Boons** to the roll, instead of gaining **+1 Boon**.
#### Elf
*Graceful and un-aging, Elves are humanoids known for their poise, pointy ears, and incredibly long life spans.*
###### Perfection
You may increase two of your Skills by 1, to a maximum of 3.
#### Dwarf
*Description Here.*
###### Shrug it Off
When you would take **Strain** from a consequence, you may instead take **3 Stress**.
#### Giant
*Description Here.*
###### Friendly Giant
When you protect your friend from a consequence, you suffer the consequence as if it the Risk was one level lower. For example, if your friend would take a Dangerous consequence, you can take a Risky consequence in their stead.
#### Goblin
*Description Here.*
###### Troublemaker
You have a knack for getting out of sticky situations. When you spend **2 Stress** to gain **+1 Boon** on a **Dangerous** Skill Check, the Risk of the check is reduced to **Risky**.
#### Orc
*Description Here.*
###### What Doesn't Kill You
When you take a **Scar**, you do not choose two Skills to reduce by 1. Instead, you choose one Skill and increase it by 1.
#### Anthron

###### Reflexes
In most situations, you are able to act before others.
#### Scalefolk
*Description Here.*
###### Natural Armour
You can take up to **4 Strain** before gaining a **Scar**, instead of 3.
#### Avia

###### Wings
Thanks to your wings, you can fly short distances with ease.
#### Angelkin
*Description Here.*
###### Helping Hand
When you spend **1 Stress** to help someone on a Skill Check and they Succeed or Critically Succeed, you clear **1 Stress**.
#### Demonkin
*Description Here.*
###### Inherited Devilry
After making a Skill Check, you may choose to gain a **Strain** in exchange for increasing your success level by one (e.g. from a partial success to a success).
#### Faeblood
*Description Here.*
###### Painted Appearance
You may spend **2 Stress** to change your appearance to that of another person for the rest of the scene, given that they are vaguely humanoid in shape and are approximately your size. You may dismiss this illusion at any time.
