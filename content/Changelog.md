### Version 1.04 // Crits & Bits
*Date: 25/02/2026*  
**Crit Thresholds**  
*By stacking multiple effects, you could get your crit threshold a little too low too easily. However I want to keep this synergy, but reduce its effectiveness. So no single effect will reduce your Crit Threshold by more than 1. Effects that previously did this have been reworked to accommodate this change.*
- Focused Attack (Adept Combat Talent)
- Analysis (Adept Trick). Still works as before, but only lasts until end of your next turn.
- Runestone of Precision (Consumable)
- Well Guided (Generic Combat Talent)
- Whispers of Truth (Prophet Miracle)

### Version 1.02 // Ancestry Rebalance, Mage Rework & Other Tweaks
*Date: 31/01/2026*  
**Ancestry Rebalance**  
Some of the Ancestries with worse features stood out as less desirable picks, and so their features have been buffed.
- Humans buffed, and now gain a Boon on making a check in which they have a 0.
- Buffed Goblin, who now treats all Dangerous Skill Checks as Risky.
- Changed Angelkin so that they are more reliably useful when helping

**Mage Rework**  
A Mage's effectiveness in battle was too easily diminished upon taking damage, especially if that damage was taken early in combat. So instead of spending Mana directly to block damage, a Mage now has a **Hex Barrier**.

The **Hex Barrier** acts as a buffer before damage eats into their Mana, which allows the Mage to more freely spend their Mana on spells.

**Other Changes**  
- New generic Minor Action: **Dodge**
  *You can now Dodge with a Minor Action, imposing +1 Bane on all attacks against you until the start of your next turn.*
- Invoker (Sanguimancy) // **Scarlet Secrets Rework**
  *Trading 1HP for 2 Mana, then using that Mana to block 4 Damage (or 2 on a melee attack), along with the Sanguimancer's Vitality Extraction allowing them to self-heal, meant that Sanguimancy was a lot tankier than it should have been. Now Scarlet Secrets trades HP for Blood Tokens, which can be used in place of Mana when casting a Spell.*
- Adepts // **Focus Point Minor Rework**
  *Previously, an Adept would spend Focus Points on their features and Tricks, gaining 2d6 for each Focus Point spent. This has been changed to simply spending Focus Dice directly, giving more granular control over how much you want to gamble. This wasn't done initially to avoid players only spending 1 Focus Die on a Trick and then failing half the time, but I now believe the more granular control is worth the trade off.*
- Prophet Weapon: **Empyrean Staff Buff**
  *Changed the vulnerable on hit from lasting until end of targets next turn to end of prophet's next turn.*
- Vanguard Move: **Combo Nerf**
  *Combo was easily the best Move, but feels really cool to use. So we're adding a slight nerf to its effectiveness by imposing a cumulative +1 Bane on the attack for each successive use.*
### Version 1.01 // Weapon Update
*Date: 16/01/2026*  
I wanted to get an equal number of Weapon options for each type. Vanguard currently has 7, so we're bringing everyone else up to that. If you want to swap weapons, you can straight away (we'll just retcon).

**Adept Weapons**  
- Buffed Whip's damage 1d6+1 -> 1d8+1 (it was kinda the worst weapon)
- Added Ornate Rapier, introducing a Deadly option for Adepts
- Nerfed Tristrike Crossbow's damage 2d4+1 -> 1d8+1 (with Graze, having a min damage of 3 was quite good and made it straight up better than many of the other adept weapons)
- Added Venom-Tipped Dart, for a consistent source of Persistent Damage

**Mage Weapons**  
- Added Psi Crystal, giving an option to dish out OFF-GUARD consistently
- Added Elderblood Shard, allowing you convert the enemy's lifeforce into a boon for your next attack or save

**Prophet Weapons**  
- Added Ball of Light, imposing OFF-GUARD on a hit
- Added Carved Bones, granting a way to dish out Weak consistently
- Added Divine Chains, the first melee weapon for Prophets. It has the deadly tag, Reach 2, and imposes STUNNED on a crit
