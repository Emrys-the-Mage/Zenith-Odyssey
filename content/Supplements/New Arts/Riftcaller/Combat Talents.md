##### Shepherd the Horde
*Prerequisite: [[Supplements/New Arts/Riftcaller/index|Riftcaller]]*

*Prerequisite: [[Supplements/New Arts/Riftcaller/index|Riftcaller]]*

*Spirit Form. 4 Mana*  
The characters you summon in this **Spell** are [[Conditions & Statuses#Invisible|INVISIBLE]].
