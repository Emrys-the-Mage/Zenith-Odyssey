##### Elemental Summons
When you conjure a Summon, you may select an element to imbue it with. Choose one of the elements below.

**FIRE**  
The Summon gains the [[Generic Amplifiers#Infernal|Infernal]] amplifier.

**ICE**  
The Summon gains the [[Generic Amplifiers#Chilling|Chilling]] amplifier.

**STONE**  
The Summon gains the [[Generic Amplifiers#Tough|Tough]] amplifier.

**STORM**  
The Summon gains the [[Generic Amplifiers#Floating|Floating]] amplifier.
##### Trait Mimicry
When you conjure a Summon and grant it an amplifier with your Elemental Summons feature, you also grant that amplifier to yourself.

This amplifier lasts until the end of the scene, or until you grant yourself another amplifier with this feature.
