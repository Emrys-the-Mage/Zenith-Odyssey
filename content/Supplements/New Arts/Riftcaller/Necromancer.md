##### Blasphemous Summons
Your Summons gain the [[Generic Amplifiers#Undead|Undead]] amplifier.
##### Recycle Undeath
When one of your Summons is [[Conditions & Statuses#DEFEATED|DEFEATED]], you regain **1 Mana** unless the Summon was a Minion. After every second Minion of yours is [[Conditions & Statuses#DEFEATED|DEFEATED]], you regain 1 Mana.
