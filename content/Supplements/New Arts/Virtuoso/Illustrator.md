#### Features
##### Mesmerising
Your **Save Target** is increased by 1.
##### Painted Cover
You begin combat with **5 || 6 || 7 Ink**.

As a Minor action, you may spend any amount of **Ink** to create the same amount of size 1 objects within Range 5. These objects act as **hard cover**, and are destroyed should they take any damage. They have an **EVASION** of 0 and fail all saves.

When you use a **Miracle** and gain its Divine Effect, you gain **1 Ink**. There is no cap on the amount of **Ink** you can hold.
