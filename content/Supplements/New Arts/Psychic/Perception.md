#### Features
##### Shared Senses
When you use a **Trick** with the Psionics tag, you may use it as if you were in one of your allies' spaces, as long as the chosen ally is within Range 10 of you.
##### Third Eye
As a Minor Action, you may temporarily open your third eye. When you do so, reduce your **HP** by 1, then you are granted the ability to see through all cover and obstructions, as well as ignore [[Conditions & Statuses#Invisible|INVISIBLE]] on other characters until the end of your turn.
