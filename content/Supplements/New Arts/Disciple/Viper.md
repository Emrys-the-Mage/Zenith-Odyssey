##### Pressure Points
When you use a *Basic Attack* with a melee weapon and hit, you may also inflict one of the following conditions until the end of the target's next turn:
- [[Conditions & Statuses#Immobilised|IMMOBILISED]]
- [[Conditions & Statuses#Off-Guard|OFF-GUARD]]
- [[Conditions & Statuses#Weak|WEAK]] **1 || 2 || 3**
##### Disabling Strike
When you score a **critical hit** with a *Basic Attack*, you also inflict [[Conditions & Statuses#Stunned|STUNNED]] onto the target.
