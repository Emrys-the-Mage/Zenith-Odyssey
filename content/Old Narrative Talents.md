# Generic
### Tier I
##### Curse of Vampirism
You have been cursed to live your life with an unholy lust for blood, becoming an undead known as a vampire.

You no longer need to eat, nor do you need to drink water. However, you are now required to regularly drink blood, not necessarily that of people, to sustain yourself.

Start a permanent Clock for your character, titled ***Bloodlust***. This clock has 10 segments.

When making any **Skill Check**, ask yourself: *"Can I see or smell **fresh blood** in this moment?"* (here the blood must be from something like an open wound, not blood that is currently within a creature). If you answer yes, tick your ***Bloodlust*** clock by 1 segment. Then, you gain **+1 Boon** to the check if the Skill is associated with **MIGHT** or **FINESSE**, or **+1 Bane** if it's associated with **WIT** or **PRESENCE**.  

When you fill your ***Bloodlust*** clock, your cravings take control. Choose one of the following options:
- **Stay.** You stay in the scene, but must immediately and constantly take violent actions against other characters with the sole goal to **drink**. This lasts until you do so, or until the next scene. Should you drink from another PC, they take a **Strain**.
- **Leave.** You leave the scene in an effort to protect those around you so that you may instead hunt for wildlife or other creatures you can find. You return in the following scene.

Regardless of your choice, the emotional trauma of losing control inflicts a **Scar**.

Your ***Bloodlust*** clock is reset to 0 in between Quests.

# Mage
### Tier I
##### Cryokinesis
You paint the world around you with ice. You can do the following.
- You can summon a few litres of ice on an object or floating near you.
- You can freeze or unfreeze a small object or a small part of an object.
- You are completely immune to any effects relating to cold environments.
##### Flight
You have learnt the magic required to grant an individual the ability to fly. You may spend **2 Stress** to give yourself or someone you touch the ability to fly for the rest of the scene.
##### Pyrokinesis
You command fire. You can do the following.
- You can summon a small flame within your hand.
- You can snuff out a small flame within a short distance.
- You can control the colour of a small flame or the direction it spreads towards.

By spending **2 Stress**, you can increase the size of the flames you can control up to that of a bonfire.
##### Telekinesis
You may spend **2 Stress** to telekinetically move an object you can see within a short distance. 

At Tier I, the maximum weight you may move is roughly that of a person. At Tier II, this becomes roughly the weight of horse and cart. At Tier III, this becomes the weight of a small building.
##### Terrakinesis
You mould the earth like clay. You may spend **2 Stress** to do one of the following.
- Instantly dig a hole in stone or dirt up to the size of a horse and cart.
- Summon a pillar of stone from the ground that is large enough to fit a person standing on top of it, and up to about 3 metres high.
- Shape the earth in some other way in which you move roughly the same amount of earth as the above to options.

# Prophet
### Tier I
##### Avian Form
*Prerequisite: [[Character Creation/Prophets/Classes/Wilder/index|Wilder]]*
You may spend **2 Stress** to transform into a small bird. When you do so, your equipment temporarily vanishes until you return to your normal form. While in this form, you can fly and gain **+1 Boon** to all rolls made to move quickly. You may stay in this form for the rest of the scene, or until you dispel it.
